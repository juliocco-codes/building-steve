# Fictional weekly review

## What changed

You led two customer calls and wrote the commercial proposal yourself. The planned pricing experiment did not run because the required data was unavailable.

## What it means

The customer work is evidence toward the current commercial-development priority. It is useful operating evidence, but two calls are not yet proof of a repeatable commercial skill. The strongest counterargument is that proposal work may remain support for someone else's sales process unless you also own the decision and outcome.

## Decision

Keep the commercial priority unchanged. Narrow next week's experiment to one pricing question that can be tested with available data.

## Next actions

1. Write the pricing question and success condition by Tuesday.
2. Ask to own the next customer follow-up from draft through outcome.
3. Add the result, not just the activity, to the decision log on Friday.
