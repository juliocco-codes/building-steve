# Weekly career review

## Inputs

- `direction.md`;
- `plan.md`;
- decision-log entries since the previous review;
- confirmed new evidence supplied by the user.

## Workflow

1. Summarize what materially changed this week.
2. Compare new evidence with the current plan and long-term direction.
3. Identify progress, drift, blocked commitments, and invalidated assumptions.
4. Separate decisions required now from questions that can wait.
5. Propose no more than three next actions.
6. Draft file updates, but do not apply them without confirmation.

## Output

### What changed

Only material facts and confirmed decisions.

### What it means

Interpretation, including the strongest counterargument.

### Decision

The decision required, or “No decision required this week.”

### Next actions

Up to three dated and specific actions.

## Boundaries

- Do not send messages or contact anyone.
- Do not turn speculation into a fact.
- Do not rewrite the long-term direction because of one difficult week.
- Do not preserve a plan that new evidence has clearly invalidated.
